# City 1999: Homeless in a Big City

> Status: idea (2026-11-22)

A possible game on top of NavKit: you are a homeless person in a big city around 1999.
You don't build a colony; you survive inside one that other people already run.
This doc lists what we already have, what is missing, and the smallest steps that give
the most. It follows [autarky.md](autarky.md): every item comes from somewhere and goes
somewhere, and nothing appears from nowhere.

---

## The core shift: from free to owned

In the forest everything is free: cut a tree, pick a berry. In a city **everything belongs
to someone**. A bench, a doorway, a can in a bin, a bed in a shelter. That tension (what
may I take, where may I sleep, who sends me away) *is* the game. Ownership is the one
genuinely new axis; most other parts reuse systems we have.

## Inventory

**Today** a mover holds three things: `carryingItem` (only during a job), `equippedTool`,
`equippedClothing`. Containers already work fully, including containers in containers.

**Proposal:** one extra slot, `equippedContainer`. A plastic bag, backpack or shopping
cart is a container item the mover wears or pushes; its contents are the inventory.

What comes for free:
- Other movers leave the contents alone: `IsItemAccessible()` already refuses items whose
  container is carried or reserved.
- Save/load, describe lines, spilling the contents when the mover dies or drops the bag:
  existing container code.
- Container capacity (`maxContents`) gives the bag-vs-cart trade-off. A cart holds a lot
  but is slow, can't take stairs or ladders, and can be stolen when parked.

Open: does every mover get one (shoppers carry groceries home), or only the player? Start
with everyone able to, few doing it.

## Trash, bins and deposit bottles

Bins do **not** refill on a timer. They fill because other people in the city ate and
drank. The trash is a trace of the city's life: busy places get full bins, quiet places
stay empty.

### The loop

1. **Consuming leaves something behind.** A soda leaves an *empty can*, a sandwich a
   *wrapper*, a beer an *empty bottle* (with deposit). One "leaves behind" field per item
   type (none exists today: eating just removes the item).
2. **The NPC gets rid of it.** Same tier pattern as bladder relief in `needs.c`
   (toilet → bush → floor): nearest bin with room → ground (litter). A trait decides who
   walks further to a bin and who drops it on the spot.
3. **The bin is a container.** Putting trash in uses `PutItemInContainer()`; full is full.
4. **The sink:** a garbage truck / street sweeper on a schedule empties bins and picks up
   litter. Gives timing: be at the bins before the truck comes.

### What falls out of it

- **Bins fill where people walk**: station, park bench, snack bar. Same signal as
  `groundwear.c` foot traffic.
- **Deposit bottles are choices for everyone.** A thrifty NPC returns their own bottle;
  a lazy one tosses it (your income); a kind one sets it *next to* the bin, like people do
  for bottle collectors.
- **Competition**: other collectors search the same bins.
- **Litter is a signal**: lots of it means no bins nearby or a run-down street. Can feed
  room/area quality and mood.
- Still autarkic: the can came from a shop that got it delivered, and goes to the truck.

## Money and exchange

- **Coin item** (one item type, stacks).
- **Exchange stations**: input → coins or coins → goods. They are stations with a service
  queue:
  - *Bottle return machine* (supermarket): deposit bottles → coins.
  - *Shop counter / snack stand*: coins → food, drink (the source of the cans above).
  - *Soup kitchen*: free food at set hours, long queue.
- **Begging / busking**: stand on a spot; passers-by give a coin with a chance that grows
  with foot traffic and the giver's mood/traits. Busking could reuse the
  [ensemble stations](../todo/ensemble-stations.md) idea: playing music at a seat, coins
  scale with traffic.
- Where do NPCs' coins come from? Keep it simple at first: city NPCs have an allowance.
  A full wage/economy loop is later.

## Ownership and access

- An `owner` field on items, furniture and zones (a mover, a "public" value, or a
  business/household id).
- Item search in jobs and needs skips owned items unless the mover is willing to steal
  (trait, desperation). Taking it is theft: witnesses, reputation, police.
- **Zones** (like stockpiles: a rectangle with rules): owner, opening hours, who may
  enter. A shop at night, a station hall after the last train, a private porch.
- **Doors** that let only some movers through. Pathfinding needs a per-mover or per-group
  passability check here; check the cost in the HPA* graph before committing to it.
- Pays off in the colony games too: "my bed", "my stash", theft. The freetime doc lists
  furniture ownership as deferred.

### Keys and locks

Access as a physical thing instead of a rule: a lock on a door, a key as an item.

- **Lock**: a door (`CELL_DOOR` exists, walkable for everyone today) gets a lock id, or none.
- **Key**: an item with the lock id it opens. It lives in the mover's inventory
  (`equippedContainer`), so it can be lost, dropped, found, stolen, given.
- **Who has a key** follows ownership: the shop owner has the shop key, a shelter gives
  you a locker key for one night.
- Everything else falls out of keys being items:
  - a *key-cutting kiosk* is a station (key + coins → copy),
  - *lockpicking* is a tool with a quality, takes time, can be seen,
  - *breaking a door* is salvage: noise, witnesses, a broken door stays open,
  - *squatting*: change the lock on an empty building and it's yours (ownership by key),
  - colony games: lock the food store so nobody eats the winter stock.

**Pathfinding cost, cheapest first:**
1. Plan as today (doors walkable for all). A mover without the key who reaches a locked
   door "tries the handle", remembers that door as closed *for itself*, and replans with
   that cell avoided. Needs only a per-search avoid list, no graph changes. Also looks
   realistic: people try doors.
2. Only if (1) wastes too many trips: a lock-group bitmask on HPA* abstract edges and a
   key bitmask per mover, checked during the search. Reachability regions would then be
   a superset (a region behind a locked door still counts as reachable), so "can I get
   there?" checks must handle a failed search.

## Stations in the city

The capability idea ([capability-based-tasks.md](../todo/architecture/capability-based-tasks.md))
fits the city without new systems, only new flags on `capabilityGrid`:

| Thing | Capability |
|---|---|
| Heating grate, warm air vent | heat source (and it really warms cells via `temperature.c`) |
| Bridge, porch, bus shelter | shelter (roof, no walls) |
| Cardboard, bench, sleeping bag, shelter bed | sleeping, levels 1-3 |
| Bin | trash in / scavenge |
| Public tap, fountain | water source (already a flag) |
| Public toilet | bladder (already furniture) |

Why stations suit this game: what you can do depends on what is standing around you, not
on which building you placed. The same "warm up" works at a grate, a burning barrel or a
shelter radiator; the same "sleep" at cardboard (1), a bench (2) or a shelter bed (3).

What's built is only the kitchen version (an on/off bitmask, two flags, a square radius
around a workshop). To make it work here we need, in short:

- **Levels** per capability (cardboard 1 vs shelter bed 3).
- **The world as provider**: bridges and porches give shelter (no sky above), grates give heat.
- **Needs use stations** (cold → nearest heat), not only recipes.
- **Reachable, not just near**: the shop's oven behind a locked door is not yours.
- **Access filter**: owner, keys, opening hours.
- **Direct action** for the player character, next to bills (colony) and needs.
- **Exchange + queue** at a station (bottle machine, snack stand).

Full list, order and the smallest proof (heat): see "Where we are (2026-11)" in
[capability-based-tasks.md](../todo/architecture/capability-based-tasks.md).

## Salvage: the city's tree-felling

"Everything comes from the world" still holds: the city is a big deposit of parts.
Taking things apart gives materials: a pallet becomes planks, a shopping cart metal,
cardboard boxes become a shelter. Salvage recipes are the city's version of cutting
trees, and they feed the same building system (a cardboard hut is a blueprint).

## City NPCs

- **Routines**: shoppers, commuters, shopkeepers, police, the garbage truck. Needs
  [schedule-system.md](../todo/schedule-system.md) (designed, not built).
- They have the same needs as the player (hunger, thirst, bladder): that is what makes
  them produce trash, queue at shops and use toilets.
- Queues: extend `ServiceKind` beyond ladders (shop, soup kitchen, bottle machine, toilet).
- Reputation / hygiene: how NPCs treat you. Later.

## Other systems we don't have yet

Beyond inventory, ownership/keys, money, trash and stations:

| System | Why the city needs it | Builds on |
|---|---|---|
| **Action queue** | "Walk to bin, search, take bottle, put in bag, walk to machine, queue, cash in" is one plan of many steps that can be interrupted and resumed | Designed for the dollhouse (not built) |
| **Player-character control** | You are one person: click to go, click a thing to use it; not bills and designations | `isDrafted`, mover-control doc |
| **Knowledge / memory per mover** | You don't know the city yet: where the shelter is, which bin is good, which door is locked for you, when the truck comes | Fog of war (survival); the "remember closed door" from keys |
| **NPCs coming and going** | A city is not a fixed colony: commuters arrive by train, shoppers leave, the night is empty. Spawn at edges/stations, despawn when they leave | Trains, `MAX_MOVERS` 10000 |
| **Social reactions** | Asking for a coin, being sent away, someone giving you food, witnesses to theft | Mood, traits |
| **Reputation / law** | Police move you on, confiscate a cart; shopkeepers remember you | Witnesses + ownership |
| **Hygiene and clothing condition** | How people react to you; worn-out clothes keep you less warm | `equippedClothing`, `floordirt` |
| **Item condition** | Found food may be spoiled; a found coat may be wet | Spoilage exists; containers |
| **Schedules / opening hours** | Shops, soup kitchen, last train, garbage truck | schedule-system.md (spec) |
| **Traffic** | Streets you can't just cross, cars as noise and danger | Later; trains as a model |

## Steps (small, each testable on its own)

1. **Trash loop** (smallest test): "leaves behind" field on food/drink, bin-or-ground tier
   for getting rid of it, bins as containers. Scenario: a bench, a bin, a snack stand,
   ten NPCs eating. Check that the bin fills where expected and litter appears when it's full.
2. **`equippedContainer`**: inventory.
3. **Coins + bottle return machine + snack stand**, with a new service-queue kind.
4. **`owner` field** + "skip owned items" in item search: property and theft.
5. **Locks and keys**: lock id on doors, key items, "try the handle and replan".
6. **More capability flags**: heat, shelter, sleeping levels, scavenging.
7. **Garbage truck** on a schedule (needs a simple schedule).
8. **City block scenario** with all of the above, then decide what's next.
